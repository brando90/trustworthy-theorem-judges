\subsection*{Bottom line}
Across the pooled 50-task / 175-artifact / 445-rating VeriBench corpus (195 old ratings plus 250 live Experiment 09 ratings; zero rater identities), fusing uniform-harness zero-demo distributional predictions from three frontier families (OpenAI \texttt{gpt-6-astra}, Google \texttt{gemini-4-argon-high}, Anthropic \texttt{claude-opus-5-5}) with an $(M+K)$ logit-pool CE calibrator clears the pre-registered bar across 50 tasks via 5-fold out-of-fold CV (\emph{Works}: fixed-HP RPS 0.0689 vs.\ 0.1186 set-aware constant, paired $-0.0497$ $[-0.0755, -0.0256]$, $p < 0.0001$; strict nested global RPS 0.0704, $p < 0.0001$), while on the 20 held-out \texttt{exp27\_split} test tasks alone the paired RPS gain vs.\ set-aware constant ($-0.0360$ $[-0.0754, +0.0018]$, $p = 0.0616$) crosses zero by 0.0018 (\emph{Promising}; zero-Opus ablation $C_{9b}$ reaches $-0.0380$ $[-0.0750, -0.0034]$, $p = 0.0322$). On identical architectures, cross-entropy (CE) decisively outperforms Pearson training both in the $(M+K)$ head and in end-to-end \texttt{Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507} Top-4-Block LoRA.
