\subsection*{Bottom line}
Across 50 disjoint VeriBench tasks (175 artifacts, 445 human ratings: 195 old plus 250 live Experiment~09 ratings; zero rater names in Experiment~28 and zero rater names or IDs here), calibrating zero-demo distributional predictions from three frontier families (OpenAI \texttt{gpt-6-astra}, Google \texttt{gemini-4-argon-high}, Anthropic \texttt{claude-opus-5-5}) plus \texttt{Qwen3-8B} logits achieves \emph{Promising} on 20 held-out \texttt{exp27\_split} test tasks: registered Global $G_9$ gets $\mathrm{RPS}=0.0729$ vs.\ $0.1088$ set-aware constant (paired $-0.0360$ $[-0.0746, +0.0011]$, $p=0.0560$), tying Experiment~27 $F_8$ ($0.0737$), while Experiment~27 set-aware $F_9$ scores $0.0659$/$0.0706$ vs.\ $C_7$ $0.0728$. Across all 50 tasks under strict H3-free 5-fold nested OOF (Amendment~2), Global reaches $0.0704$ ($0/10{,}000$ bootstrap draws $\ge 0$ vs.\ $0.1186$ constant, Monte Carlo floor $0.0002$; $-0.0076$, $p=0.0364$ vs.\ H3-free $F_3$; $-0.0044$ $[-0.0098, +0.0012]$, $p=0.1150$ vs.\ H3-exposed $F_8$), and post-hoc pure all-frontier global (zero \texttt{Qwen3}, zero H3, zero \texttt{is\_new}) reaches $0.0685$ ($p=0.0036$ vs.\ $F_3$, $p=0.0214$ vs.\ $F_8$). Under Priority~2 vs.\ 4, \texttt{Qwen3-4B} LoRA \texttt{CE} beats \texttt{Pearson} on both splits ($p=0.0002$), whereas matched-grid $(M+K)$ head \texttt{CE} beats \texttt{Pearson} on CE ($p=0.0014$) and TVD ($p=0.0094$) with OOF RPS crossing zero ($p=0.0786$).
