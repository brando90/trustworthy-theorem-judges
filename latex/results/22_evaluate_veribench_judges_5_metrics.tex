\subsection*{Bottom line}
Re-scoring the cached VeriBench selected judge (\texttt{H2\_sol + H2\_opus}: gpt-6-sol-high and claude-opus-5-5, Vals gateway,
6 calls per output, no differential testing, \$0 in new API calls) on the five-metric panel shows that its correlations
rose across train, validation and test (Spearman 0.6400, 0.8800, 0.8257; Pearson 0.6576, 0.8601, 0.8578) while its test
ranked probability score degraded from validation (0.1285) to test (0.1954 [0.1246, 0.2669]). That is no better than
predicting the constant training marginal (0.1836; paired difference $+0.0118$ $[-0.0898, +0.1085]$, p-val=0.8244; 30 outputs,
10 tasks). A $+0.1811$ $[+0.0989, +0.2622]$ leniency bias, 55.0\% of the test mean squared error, is invisible to correlation.

\subsection*{Other panels (as stated in the experiment's results.md)}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Across the 8-judge frontier pool, \texttt{H0\_sol} beats every \texttt{H2} single judge on test RPS (0.1907 vs 0.1959 to 0.2078) despite lower correlations (0.664/0.591 vs 0.873/0.921).
  \item Raw $\F{4}$ RPS-trained mixtures beat $\F{2}$ Pearson-trained mixtures on canonical test RPS (0.1443 vs 0.1693, difference $+0.0250$ $[+0.0063, +0.0427]$, p-val=0.0074), while tying across 1,000 random 20/5 splits ($+0.0051$ $[-0.0034, +0.0136]$).
  \item Probability-floor sensitivity: CE moves by 4+ nats between floors $10^{-6}$ and $10^{-3}$ on the 6-call histograms; RPS moves by 0.0012.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Caveats}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Post-hoc re-evaluation of cached judge scores on 195 expert ratings of 75 outputs (25 tasks); 10 test tasks, so intervals are wide.
  \item Not Lean-checked proofs and no differential testing: human formal-specification-coverage ratings only.
  \item CE depends strongly on the zero-bin smoothing floor with 6 discrete calls; RPS and ordering are stable.
\end{itemize}
