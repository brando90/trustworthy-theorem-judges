\subsection*{Bottom line}
On the 20 held-out tasks the pre-registered primary head does not clear the bar. Its ranked probability score (RPS) is 0.0698 versus 0.1088 for the set-aware constant, paired difference $-0.0390$ with 95\% task-bootstrap interval $[-0.0721, -0.0087]$ (10,000 draws, seed 20261127, 70 artifacts). Total variation distance (TVD) is also lower (0.363 versus 0.479), but cross-entropy (CE) is worse (1.249 versus 1.159) because the unpenalized RPS fit did not converge. The fixed experiment-27 head, refit on this 445-rating snapshot, stays at RPS 0.0737, paired $-0.0351$ $[-0.0764, +0.0020]$.

The H3-free 50-task nested head does clear the bar. Every fold selected the same features (two H2 means, three H4 expected scores, four structural counts). Pooled RPS is 0.0684 versus 0.1186, paired $-0.0502$ $[-0.0726, -0.0290]$. CE and TVD intervals are also entirely below 0. Pooled bias is $+0.023$ (judge mean 3.764 versus human mean 3.742). This split was already scored in experiments 27 and 28. It is not a sealed first look.

\subsection*{Caveats}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The 20-task split and the 50-task folds were already used in experiments 27 and 28.
  \item The primary argmin did not converge. The written rule therefore says not found, even though its RPS interval lies below 0.
  \item Dropping 32 blank attestations does not pull the F8 interval below 0. Keeping only schema attestations does, and that is a different target.
  \item One \texttt{candidate\_file} cell is free text. The join uses \texttt{rater\_id} and \texttt{item\_id}. The three scores on that item are all 5, so the histogram does not change if that row is dropped.
  \item No rater names in this fragment.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Appendix: method}
Cumulative logit \(P(r \le k) = \mathrm{sigmoid}((\theta_k - w \cdot x) / T)\), fit by CE or by RPS, plus \((\alpha/2) \lVert w \rVert^2\), mixture weight \(m \in \lbrace 0, 0.25, 0.5 \rbrace\). Selection uses validation RPS only. Correlation is not a training objective. Cached features only: H2, H3, H4 and structural counts. H3 is excluded from the 50-task pass. CPU, SciPy 1.18.1, \$0, no new model call. Full tables, old and new cohorts, and the attestation check are in the experiment \texttt{results.md}.
