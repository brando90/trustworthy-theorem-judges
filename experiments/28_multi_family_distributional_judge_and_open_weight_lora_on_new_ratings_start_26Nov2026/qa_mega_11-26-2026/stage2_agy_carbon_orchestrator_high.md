### `[MAJOR]` A2. Scale-invariance vs. L2-shrinkage collapse invalidates the `(M+K)` head Pearson numbers
- **Evidence:** In [distlib.py](file:///Users/brandomiranda/trustworthy-theorem-judges/experiments/28_multi_family_distributional_judge_and_open_weight_lora_on_new_ratings_start_26Nov2026/scripts/distlib.py#L279-L309), `MKCalibrator(objective="pearson")` minimizes $-\mathrm{Corr}(\mu(\theta), y) + \frac{\alpha_a}{2}\|a_0\|^2 + \frac{\alpha}{2}\|\tau, w\|_2^2$. Because Pearson correlation is scale-invariant and $\mu(\theta)$ is linear in $\theta$ near $0$, any CE-scale L2 penalty ($\alpha \in \{0.3, 1, 3, 10\}$) drives $\theta \to 0$ until hitting the non-smooth floor `smu <= 1e-8` (`distlib.py:284`).
- **Recomputed verification (`cv5_oof` on `C7`):**
  - Reported (`alpha = 0.3`): $\|\theta\|_2 = [0.235, 0.027, 0.253, 0.207, 0.210]$, $\mathrm{sd}(E) = [0.066, 0.0017, 0.090, 0.078, 0.034]$ -> collapses near uniform (`mean_E = 2.382`, `RPS = 0.2325`, `CE = 1.8371`, `ICC(A,1) = 0.046`). The wild fold-to-fold scale collapse (`0.0017` on fold 1 vs `0.090` on fold 2) also breaks `head_pearson_postcal` (`RPS = 0.1203`, worse than constant `0.1186`).
  - With small regularization (`alpha = 1e-4`, `alpha_a = 0.05`): $\|\theta\|_2 \in [3.9, 7.4]$, $\mathrm{sd}(E) \in [0.95, 1.64]$ -> raw Pearson head improves dramatically from `RPS = 0.2325` to **`RPS = 0.0806`, `CE = 1.1314`, `TVD = 0.3621`, `ICC(A,1) = 0.860`, `r = 0.860`**!
- **Implication:** CE (`RPS = 0.0689`, `CE = 0.922`) still beats Pearson cleanly, but reporting `head_pearson_raw = 0.2325` and `head_pearson_postcal = 0.1203` as a matched Priority 2 vs. 4 result is scientifically indefensible because it measures an L2-vs-scale-invariance bug rather than Pearson optimization.
