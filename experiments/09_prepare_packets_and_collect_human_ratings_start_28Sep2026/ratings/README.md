**Checked: 11-11-2026.** The collection still contains **15 numeric warm-up ratings and three N/A reference-issue entries; no completed main ratings**. Preparing the new packet added no observations. CSV means comma-separated value file; these are saved snapshots of the Sheet response tables, not a live mirror.

Warm-up and current main forms share eleven columns. Final score and the combined artificial intelligence (AI) assistance/human-rating attestation are required for new ratings. Time and confidence are requested; a one-sentence rationale is **preferred but optional**; problem notes are for issues. Names and IDs are prefilled metadata. Participant responses retain blanks, capitalization, spelling and N/A entries; assistant-estimated times and assistant-written background annotations were removed. ## Derived analysis files

[analysis/](analysis/) contains separately labeled historical normalized imports. Those imports lowercase some answers and omit nonnumeric rows. They are not raw submissions and must not be counted again. `refresh_named_ratings.py` writes only those analysis exports by default, never these primary raw tables.

Original snapshots and withdrawn coordinator additions remain in the audit history. Refreshes must preserve exact response values and missingness; do not infer per-item times or fill other responses.
